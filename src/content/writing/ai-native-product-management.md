## AI changes the operating model

Adding a conversational surface can be useful, but it does not necessarily make a product or a team AI-native.

## Look at the work, not only the interface

The larger opportunity is to rethink workflows, ownership, and decision boundaries around what machines can now do. This shifts product strategy from feature placement toward system design.

## Design the boundary

Strong AI product work makes clear what the system can decide, what evidence it needs, and when responsibility returns to a person.
