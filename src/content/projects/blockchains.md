## Overview

Served as Senior Product Manager for Equs, a Web3 social and metaverse product for creators building virtual worlds.

## Product approach

Led cross-functional work across engineering, UX, and marketing to establish feature parity between web and mobile for the MVP. The work included third-party identity verification, product-roadmap management, stakeholder alignment, and coordinated delivery of the core platform experience.
