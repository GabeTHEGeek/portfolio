## Overview

Co-founded AudioStaq and led product for an enterprise podcast hosting and monetization platform spanning content management, ad operations, analytics, distribution, APIs, schemas, and reporting.

## Product approach

The work connected creator-facing tools with the operational and technical systems required to host, monetize, measure, and distribute audio. The product consolidated publishing, performance metrics, audience engagement, advertising, and monetization into a single workflow for creators and brands.
