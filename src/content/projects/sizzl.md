## Overview

Led product for Sizzl, a live-streaming platform built for culinary creators and food communities across iOS, Android, and web.

## Product approach

The work focused on taking an ambiguous 0→1 concept through beta launch and coordinated multi-platform delivery. The product brought live video, co-streaming, audience reactions, recipes, notifications, Q&A, scheduling, and monetization into one creator workflow.
