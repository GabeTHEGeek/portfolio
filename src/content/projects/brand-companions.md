## Overview

BrandCompanionsAI began as RedditLeads, an AI-powered companion designed to help brands contribute useful, on-brand responses to real Reddit conversations. It scans for high-intent keywords, drafts responses, and can be customized by vertical, tone, and targeting rules.

## Product approach

Originally built to support WarrantyPilot's early traction, the experiment considers how a brand can remain recognizable and useful when its primary interaction becomes a conversation.

## Key lessons

Conversational interfaces need a clear product role and interaction model; personality alone is not a product strategy.
