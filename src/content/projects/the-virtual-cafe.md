## Overview

I love calm games that give you a place to settle in and enjoy being around other people without pressure. The Virtual Café brings that feeling to a shared coworking room. Pick an animal avatar, find a seat, put on music, and work alongside friends or new faces.

## The experience

Visitors can switch between a bright day café and a warmer night scene, set a Pomodoro timer, and see who else is around. Chat, profiles, invitations, and a guestbook make it easy to connect. Little gestures, like sending someone a snack or sharing a postcard, give the space its personality.

## Why I built it

I wanted to explore what networking could feel like if it took its cues from cozy games instead of feeds and pitches. The idea is simple: make a place where getting something done and meeting someone new can happen naturally.
